# Brand foundation — Afro Arab Properties

## Essence

**Attainable ownership with titled confidence.** Afro Arab helps Ghanaian families and diaspora investors buy land and homes through transparent projects, interest-free payment plans, and construction delivery they can verify.

## Positioning

| Axis | Stance |
| --- | --- |
| Promise | Everyone can buy a house — when inventory, title process, and payment plans are clear |
| Differentiator | Interest-free payment plans + developer delivery across Accra corridors |
| Proof | Completed/ongoing projects, awards, title documentation, named leadership |
| Tone | Confident, concrete, local; never generic luxury fluff |

## Audiences

1. **First-time Ghanaian home buyers** — need clarity on deposit, schedule, title, location
2. **Diaspora investors** — need trust signals, progress evidence, remote enquiry paths
3. **Land buyers** — need plot availability, size, facing, access roads, legal status
4. **Commercial seekers** — secondary; keep separate from residential journey

## Brand pillars

1. **Clarity** — exact units, prices, statuses, dates
2. **Access** — payment plans that make ownership reachable
3. **Place** — Ghanaian locations, materials, climate, community
4. **Delivery** — construction quality and handover evidence
5. **Stewardship** — titled land process and honest availability

## Voice

- Prefer: “30 three-bedroom townhouses in Community 22 Annex, Zenu”
- Avoid: “elevate”, “seamless”, “unlock your dream lifestyle”
- Currency: always `GH¢` with full numbers or clear ranges
- Status vocabulary: Available / On hold / Reserved / Sold / Coming soon / Sold out

## Visual direction (summary)

Warm Ghanaian modernism: forest green, warm red accent, sand/stone neutrals, architectural photography, site-plan line work. Full token system lives in [design-system.md](design-system.md).

## Proof requirements before publish

- Company registration / regulator references
- Award sources with dates
- Title/permit documents or clear process copy
- Project last-updated dates
- Construction progress photos with dates
- Verified inventory counts (never placeholder thousands)
